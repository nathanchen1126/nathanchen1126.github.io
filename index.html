About

I am a PhD student in the School of Public Affairs at Zhejiang University, where I joined the Interdisciplinary Training Program in September 2024. I am supervised by Prof. Wenze Yue. My co-supervisors in the program are Prof. Weiwen Zhang, Dr. Yongping Zhang, and Prof. Nenggan Zheng, all at Zhejiang University.

Recently, I have begun studying how temporally compounding climate extremes affect crop production and food-system resilience. I combine daily climate data, gridded crop records and climate-model scenarios with spatial econometric analysis and machine learning, focusing on the order and timing of heatwaves and heavy rainfall, differences across major crops and regions, and possible adaptation pathways.

Previously, I received my B.A. from the School of Public Administration at Central China Normal University in 2024, supervised by Prof. Xinhai Lu.
