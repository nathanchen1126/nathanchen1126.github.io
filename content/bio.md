I am a PhD student in the [School of Public Affairs at Zhejiang University](https://www.spa.zju.edu.cn/), where I joined the Interdisciplinary Training Program in September 2024. I am supervised by Prof. Wenze Yue.

My research interests span **climate change, land use planning and management, spatial governance, and urban sustainability**. My current work examines temporal compound climate extremes and agricultural adaptation, alongside the geography of agricultural land sharing.

Previously, I received my B.A. from the School of Public Administration at [Central China Normal University](https://www.ccnu.edu.cn/) in 2024, supervised by Prof. Xinhai Lu.
