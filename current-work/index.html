Current Work

TCE · Climate risk and adaptation

Temporally compounding climate extremes and crop production

My current climate research asks how heatwaves and heavy rainfall in close succession shape risks to global crop production. Using daily climate records, gridded crop data, crop calendars and climate-model scenarios, I examine heat-followed-by-rain and rain-followed-by-heat sequences for maize, rice, soybean and wheat. Spatial econometric models and machine learning help assess associations between event timing, crop-season exposure and production. The next stage will explore locally feasible crop calendars, irrigation and drainage, and crop portfolios to understand where response measures may reduce shortfalls and where residual risk remains.

PV · Solar energy and land sharing

Cultivation evidence redraws China’s agrivoltaic land-sharing geography

My agrivoltaics research examines where solar infrastructure and farming are observably combined. I integrate high-resolution imagery, Sentinel-2 vegetation phenology and machine-learning classification to identify cultivation within photovoltaic arrays across China, then compare this activity-based evidence with project records and facility-based inventories. The analysis examines how these evidence choices change spatial patterns, county priorities for verification, modelled generation potential and a vegetation-based grain-equivalent proxy. Its central finding is that similar national totals can conceal markedly different regional geographies of land sharing.
